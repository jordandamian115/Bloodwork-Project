# Laposata Clinical Laboratory Reference Values (AccessMedicine)

- Source id: `accessmedicine-lab-values`
- URL: https://accessmedicine.mhmedical.com/content.aspx?bookid=2503&sectionid=201361245
- Cluster: hormones
- Teaching target: Tabulated ranges for cortisol by time of day, estradiol by sex/status, testosterone, SHBG-related, homocysteine, magnesium.

Live AccessMedicine content is paywalled (HTTP 403 on ingest). Do not store copyrighted McGraw Hill tables.

Use these public stand-ins already in this corpus:

- NCBI Bookshelf normal ranges (`ncbi-normal-ranges`) for metabolic, thyroid, FSH/LH, magnesium, lipids
- MedlinePlus CMP, homocysteine, magnesium, testosterone
- PMC steroid diagnostics (`pmc-steroid-diagnostics`) for LC-MS/MS steroid ranges and time-of-day cortisol caveats
- Endotext testicular function chapter for SHBG and free/bioavailable testosterone

Public teaching notes commonly cited alongside that chapter (confirm against the live lab report):

- Homocysteine typical adult band often quoted around 4–12 µmol/L (method-dependent)
- Cortisol interpretation is clock-time dependent (morning vs afternoon/evening)
- Estradiol intervals are sex- and cycle/status-specific
- Magnesium and homocysteine must be interpreted with the performing lab's interval
